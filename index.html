Manage My Store — Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Manage My Store is a billing, stock and staff app for shops. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: the shop's information stays on the shop's own devices. The developer of the app does not receive, store, sell or share it.

Who is responsible for the data

Each shop that uses the app decides what to record and keeps its own data. The shop owner is responsible for the customer and staff information they enter. The developer has no access to it.

What the app stores

  • Shop records: products, prices, stock, sales, returns, purchases, suppliers and expenses.
  • Customers (optional): name, phone number and address, when the shop chooses to save them, for example for credit sales or tax invoices (buyer name and PAN).
  • Staff accounts: name, username, phone (optional), and passwords and PINs stored only as secure one-way hashes.
  • Activity log: which staff member did what and when, so the owner can check the shop's records.

Where it is stored and sent

All of this is stored on the device the shop chooses as its main device (a Windows PC or an Android device). Staff phones paired with it keep a working copy and send their sales to the main device over the shop's own Wi-Fi, encrypted with HTTPS. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any cloud service run by the developer.

Backups are encrypted with the shop's backup password and saved on the main device; the owner may copy them to a USB drive or a cloud drive of their choice.

Internet use

The app works on the local network. When it makes a PDF (receipts, labels, reports) it may download font files from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com); like any web request this reveals the device's IP address to Google, but no shop data is sent. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan barcodes and the pairing QR code. No pictures are saved or sent.
  • Network, Wi-Fi and multicast: to find and connect to the main device on the shop Wi-Fi.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep the main device's server running and show that it is running.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock (optional): handled by the phone itself; the app never sees fingerprint or face data.

Deleting accounts and data

  • The shop owner can turn off or remove staff accounts in the app (Staff), and remove paired devices (Devices). Removing a device wipes its working copy the next time it connects.
  • To delete everything on a phone, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • To delete all shop data, the owner deletes the app's data folder on the main device and its backups.
  • Sales and accounting records that a shop must keep by law are the shop's responsibility to retain or delete.

Because the developer holds no copy of any shop's data, the developer cannot view, export or delete it on anyone's behalf.

Children

The app is meant for shop owners and staff aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.
